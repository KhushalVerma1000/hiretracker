2. Who this policy covers

3. Our role

The customer decides what candidate and client data to enter and why. JopUP processes that data on the customer's behalf and instructions. Candidates who want access, correction or deletion should contact the agency that holds their data first. If you cannot reach them, write to us at [Email] and we will help.

4. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, phone, role, organisation, and a password hash (never the password).
  • Customer-entered data: candidate names, contact details, resumes, employment details, pipeline stage and notes; client contacts and open positions.
  • Usage and audit data: login times, actions taken and IP address where logged.
  • Email-connection data (only if you connect a mailbox): the connected email address, OAuth tokens, granted permissions, connection status, and a log of each message sent through the connection (time, recipients, subject, message ID; not the message body).

We do not ask for, and customers should not enter without need, sensitive details such as caste, religion or health information.

5. How we use data

To provide and secure the service, give support and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Google user data

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, JopUP requests one mailbox permission, send-only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. This lets JopUP send an email from your Gmail address when you click Send. We do not request permission to read, list, search, modify or delete any email, draft, label, contact or attachment in your Gmail account. We also request the basic openid and email permissions, which only tell us which Gmail address you connected.

What we access and store. When you connect, we store your Gmail address, the OAuth refresh token (encrypted at rest), the permissions you granted and the connection status. For each email you send through the connection we keep a record of the time, recipients, subject and message ID. We do not store the contents of your Gmail mailbox.

How we use it. Only to send the emails you ask us to send, from your own address, when you click Send in JopUP. Each send is a single message triggered by you. We never send mail automatically through your account.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent or share Google user data. We do not transfer it to others except as necessary to provide the send feature you asked for, to comply with law, or for security. We do not use it for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based advertising.

Human access. Our staff do not read your data. We access it only with your explicit consent for a specific item, where needed for security, or where the law requires it.

Disconnecting and deletion. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, Email accounts. We then revoke the token with Google where possible and delete the stored connection. You can also remove JopUP's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have remaining records deleted, email [Email].

JopUP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Microsoft user data

If you connect Outlook or Microsoft 365, JopUP requests the delegated permissions Mail.Send and offline_access, plus basic openid and email permissions that only identify the connected mailbox. We do not request read access to your mailbox and do not use application permissions. We store the connected address, the granted permissions, the connection status and the latest refresh token (encrypted at rest; Microsoft rotates it on use). We keep the same send log as above and never the message body. Use is limited to sending messages you ask us to send; we do not sell or share this data or use it for advertising. You can disconnect in Settings, Email accounts, or remove access at myaccount.microsoft.com.
